# Paster Dream Reborn v0.2.0-beta-hotfix1 (Forge 1.20.1)

Version: `0.2.0-forge-1.20.1-beta-hotfix1`

This is a hotfix release for v0.2.0-beta. It adds two Shadow World challenge advancements, a Dream Train Ticket dimension whitelist config and a Dream Fertilizer recipe, and updates the content regulations.

## Added

**Advancements & Story**
- Added the challenge advancement "Was it me who killed me?!", earned by marking a Wailing Shadow Ghost with the Conflict Prophecy Card
- Added the challenge advancement "Returned to Its Rightful Owner...?", earned by crafting the Lost Sword Tomb

**Recipes**
- Added a Mortar recipe for Dream Fertilizer: Pink Slimeball + Bone Block yields 9 Dream Fertilizer, with the Mortar not consumed

**Features**
- Added the `trainTicketDimensionWhitelist` config for the Dream Train Ticket, defaulting to the three vanilla dimensions plus the mod's Dyedream World, Lamp Shadow World and Wind Journey
- Using the ticket in a dimension outside the whitelist now shows "The Dream Train has no station here, unable to stop"
- The dimension whitelist is cached for faster validation and supports config hot-reloading

## Docs
- Updated the content regulations: renamed "Addon Mod Regulations" to "Content Regulations" and extended them to all content published using this mod as its subject or medium, with the Chinese and English wording aligned
