# Changelog

## v1.0.0-beta1 (Forge 1.20.1)

Version: `1.0.0-forge-1.20.1-beta1`

### Added

- Added a note preview screen to the Rebirth Dream Crystal, which lists read notes by Dyedream World, Under the Lamp Shadow, and Wind Journey; locked notes display "This note has not been unlocked yet" and cannot be opened, and pages can be turned by clicking the arrows or scrolling the mouse wheel
- Added dimension quests to the Rebirth Dream Crystal: Dyedream World requires reading 30 notes and submitting 1 Melt Dream Crystal Fragment; Under the Lamp Shadow requires reading 16 notes, killing 200 shadow creatures, and submitting 1 Pure Horror; Wind Journey requires reading 7 notes, completing "Angel Beats!", and submitting 1 Pulse Windrunner Crystal
- Added quest reward attributes to the Rebirth Dream Crystal: completing the Dyedream World quest grants +16 max health and +16 Melt Dream Aura; completing the Under the Lamp Shadow quest grants +16% Skill Damage and +16 armor; completing the Wind Journey quest grants +16% movement speed and -16% skill cooldown
- Added the Potion Bottle of Cyclone, which on impact launches all non-player entities within an 8x8 area at the landing spot upward and applies Broken Wing, gaining 1 level per 20 max health (up to 5), with each level increasing fall damage taken by 50%, for 10 seconds
- Added a Dream Cauldron recipe for the Potion Bottle of Cyclone using 100 mB Melt Dream Spring, 125 mB Guiding Drug (fluid), Wind Cleaving Grass, Congeal Wind, and a Feather
- Added the Snowball (Stuffed with Stone), thrown by right-click, dealing 3 damage on hit and consuming 1 per throw in Survival mode
- Added a shapeless recipe for the Snowball (Stuffed with Stone) using a vanilla Snowball and 1 Pebble
- Slumber Palm Trees now generate on the pool banks of Wind Journey with a 20% chance of 1-2 trees, and Slumber Palm Saplings can be planted on Sand
- Added Slumber Palm Club Soda, which restores 3 hunger and 0.5 saturation and grants Speed I for 60 seconds when consumed, and can be eaten at any hunger level
- Added a shapeless recipe for Slumber Palm Club Soda using Wind Feather Grass, Slumber Palm, and a Glass Cup
- Added a 30-second Cook effect to the Martini Glass of Sea Salt Slumber Palm Beverage
- Added the "Homelands in a Dream" advancement, completed after visiting all 16 biomes of the Dyedream World, rewarding 100 experience
- Added three full-set armor advancements: "Woven of Dream" for wearing the full Melt Dream Crystal set, "Cloaked in Shadow" for the full Shadow Alloy set, and "Gale-Borne" for the full Fluffy Wind Alloy set
- Added the Dreamseeker Notes "Search Record About Dyedream Tree" and "Search Notes About Wind Journey World's Mob", placed in the book structures atop the Worldtree and in the hot-air balloon respectively
- Added a crafting recipe for the Shadow Hand Lantern using Chains, Glass Panes, a Pink Candle, Black Metal Nuggets, and a Black Metal Ingot
- Added a Mortar recipe for Flour, which can be ground from 2 Wheat, 2 Rye Seeds, or 4 Wheat Seeds
- Added 27 Create compatibility recipes, covering crushing, milling, cutting, and heated mixing in a Basin
- Added 54 Immersive Engineering compatibility recipes, covering Arc Furnace smelting, Garden Cloche planting, the Crusher, and the Sawmill
- Added Botany Pots and Botany Trees compatibility: 6 crops can be planted on Dyedream Dirt, Dyedream Saplings can be planted in Botany Trees pots, and Dyedream Dirt, Dyedream Grass Blocks, and Snow Blocks can be used as pot soil
- Added Wandering Trader trade injection, appending 2 mod trades to the end of each trader's offers by default; the number is configurable, and trades can instead be injected into the vanilla trade pool
- Added a dedicated trade table for Wandering Traders in the Dyedream World, consisting of vanilla base trades, 13 common trades, and 4 exclusive trades offering the Dyedream Deep Treasure, Life Crystal, Yinhul Cotton Candy, and Dream Cauldron
- Wandering Traders now spawn naturally in the Dyedream World using the Overworld spawn timer and probability, accompanied by 2 Llamas
- Piglins now accept Molten Gold Ingots for bartering, with a dedicated loot table
- Added a Deep Sea Treasure trade to Master Fishermen, exchanging 16-32 Emeralds for 1 Deep Sea Treasure
- Injected Molten Gold items into 5 vanilla Nether chest loot tables, which can yield Molten Gold Nuggets, Molten Gold Ingots, Golden Apples, and Enchanted Golden Apples
- Added note fragment drops from shadow creatures, which have an 80% chance to drop an extra Lamp Shadow Broken Note on death
- Added a crafting table recipe for Molten Gold Ingots using 4 Blaze Powder and 1 Gold Ingot
- Molten Gold Ingots can now be used as fuel, burning for 6400 ticks
- Molten Gold and Fluffy Wind Alloy items are no longer destroyed by lava
- Added enchanting table sounds when activating the Soul Gem
- Added the "Require Pliers or Shears for Harvesting" config option, enabled by default; when disabled, plants can be harvested with an empty hand
- Added the "Challenges" category and the "Angel Beats!" entry to the guide book
- Added a value-jitter config option for Sanity Bar Preset 2, enabled by default
> Note: The Rebirth Dream Crystal cannot currently be obtained in normal survival.

### Changed

- Dyedream Cracks now let creative-mode players teleport directly without unlocking the advancement
- Sword-qi blade light now uses texture rendering instead of procedural geometry, so the blade light and trails are visible from a distance
- Unified the "Gale Passage" passive of Fluffy Wind Alloy tools and the Windwreathed Thunder Spear: attacks deal additional lightning damage equal to 10% of attack damage, and the higher the movement speed, the higher the damage and mining speed
- Adjusted the Break Wind Curtain pass counter to count only while Elytra gliding; diagonal passes no longer count twice
- Adjusted the Melt Dream Crystal Chest Reset Tool so the loot list is shown only while holding Shift
- Enabled the health replacement mechanic when SAN is disabled: Curse of Heart-Devouring increases that instance of damage by an additional 50% x level, Heart-Devouring Repair now consumes 1 health to repair 1 durability, and the Shadow Sword and Seal of the Corrupted refuse to be used and drop
- Foxfire and Shaking Crystal are now normal entities with no health, and can no longer be attacked or killed
- The note name list, note screen text, and the biome list on the JEI Dream Accumulator recipe page now scroll automatically
- Added names for over 20 entities, including the Lightning Bolt, Thrown Pink Egg, Windwreathed Thunder Spear, Tide Sword qi, and Kusanagi's Venomous Stinger
- The "The Dust Has Settled" advancement is now granted the first time the Dream Accumulator screen is opened
- Added the "Lightning in a Bottle" advancement unlock condition to the guide book's "Wind-Breaking Knight" entry
- Moved the Glass Jar of Wind Plant Extract to the Food & Drink creative tab, and the Slumber Palm blocks to the Wind Journey tab
- Completed common Forge tags: added storage block and ore tags for the Congeal Wind Block, Windrunner Crystal Block, Raw Titanium Block, Charged Amethyst Block, and Soul Ore; added fruit tags for Dyedream Fruit, Slumber Palm, and Fig; added the mycelium placeable tag for shadow plants
- Added 6 English translations to the Dreamseeker Notes
- Added 7 acquisition methods for plant items to JEI
- Molten Shadow now behaves like water: a new source block forms when there are two adjacent source blocks next to a flowing block and a solid block below, and it is controlled by the "Water Source Conversion" game rule

### Balance

- Adjusted Shadow Breath: health regeneration now applies when Sanity is at or below 40%, increasing by 1 level for every further 10% Sanity lost, up to IV; it now drops from each of the 3 chest types in the Shadow Dungeon Repository with a 1% chance each, and its rarity is raised to Ancient
- Increased the max health bonus of the Red Dew Ring from +1/+2/+3/+4 to +4/+6/+8/+10 at levels I-IV
- Reduced the drying time of Coarse Salt from 24000 ticks to 6000 ticks
- Extended the Frenzy duration of the Potion Bottle of Berserk from 3 seconds to 30 seconds
- Changed the Black Metal compression ratio from 4:1 to 9:1, and added reverse recipes: 1 ingot into 9 grains and 1 block into 9 ingots
- Increased the Melt Dream Coin drops of Dyedream Ruins chests, Shadow Ruins chests, and Melt Dream Crystal treasure chests: Dyedream Ruins rare chests from 1-3 to 2-6, and Shadow Ruins common chests from a fixed 1 to a fixed 3
- Added Fortune bonuses to Dyedream Buds: small, medium, and large buds drop an extra 0-2, 1-3, and 2-4 respectively

### Fixed

- Fixed the Dyedream Budding Block dropping itself without Silk Touch; it now drops only with Silk Touch
- Fixed an out-of-bounds read when note progress data is missing
- Fixed mod ranged weapons firing Spectral Arrows not triggering the empowered shot while still consuming Melt Dream Energy
- Fixed the Melt Dream Bow and Fluffy Wind Bow not zooming the view while drawing
- Fixed the collision and selection boxes of Pink Mushroom and Shadow Fungus blocks not following their random offsets
- Fixed Bone Meal duplicating Shroomlight when growing Pink Mushrooms, and increased the Shroomlight generation chance of giant Pink Mushrooms from 25% to 40%
- Fixed the incorrect description of the enchantment bonus in the Kusanagi tooltip
- Fixed wrong block IDs in the Nine-Tailed Fox garden decryption structure
- Fixed high-speed sword-qi occasionally missing hits and not triggering invulnerability frames after a hit
- Fixed the generation position of the Melt Dream Crystal Chest in the Moon House
- Fixed the Reed Rod paper-making recipe and the Ferraria Crispa, Eustoma, and Malva Sinensis Cavan dye recipes overriding vanilla recipes
- Fixed Skeleton Trap Horses generating in the Dyedream World
- Fixed common tool tooltips displaying above vanilla attributes
- Fixed the Debug Sword and Debug Tool deleting the player
- Fixed a crash when clicking the fluid slot of the Mortar in the release build
- Fixed the Kaichu Omamori displaying incorrect information when Melt Dream Energy is insufficient, and changed its health regeneration duration to 15s

### Removed

- Removed the guide book "Story" category and its 6 entries: Deposition Shadow, Lamp Shadow Travelogue I, Shadow Dungeon, Deception, The Bargain, and Shattered
- Removed the Dreamseeker Notes "Daedalus' Wings and the Floating Island"

### Renamed

- Changed the English name of the Lightning Bolt from Thundercloud to Lightning Bolt

### Technical

- Switched LivingEntityAccessor and boss health bar reads to access transformers
- Unified tool passive logic into a single Mixin dispatch, removing the duplicate implementations in individual tool classes
- Added a common interface to Molten Gold and Melt Dream Crystal tools for addon mods to extend
- Made 4 tool helper classes, including MeltDreamToolHelper, public for addon mods to call
- Replaced the HashMap in FMLCommonSetup with ConcurrentHashMap
- Unified the storage bag module's package name to all lowercase
- Fixed ID typos in two entities and added legacy ID compatibility mappings
- Unified the namespace of some recipe IDs
- Unified line endings to CRLF and added .editorconfig
