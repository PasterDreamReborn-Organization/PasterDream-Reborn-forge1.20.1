# Paster Dream Reborn v0.2.1 (Forge 1.20.1)

Version: `0.2.1-forge-1.20.1`

## ✨ Overview

This update adds the Windwreathed Thunder Spear, White Sandstone / Dyedream Sandstone building sets, the Slumber Palm Block and the Wind Moor Sapling, polishes the Cook Overflow, protected-effect and SAN change-rate display mechanics, provides brand-new loot for Dyedream Campsites, and rebalances weapon stats and recipes, along with a large batch of fixes.

### Added

**Weapons & Items**
- Added the endgame-tier polearm "Windwreathed Thunder Spear": left-click melee deals lightning damage; right-click charges a dash (a Riptide-style forward lunge); Shift+Right-click charges a phantom throw that triggers chain lightning on hit and flies back automatically; it carries the "Thunder Follows the Wind" passive (the faster you move, the more damage you deal; drops are swept to your side by the wind); the dash and throw consume Melt Dream Energy
- Added Moltengold material assets and a legacy texture pack
- Chocolate now switches to the storage cell texture when renamed to "Storage Cell" / "storage cell"
- Split the deprecated Dreamseeker Notes into a new creative-mode tab

**Blocks & Building**
- Added the White Sandstone building set: White Sandstone, Cut / Chiseled / Smooth White Sandstone, and their matching stairs, slabs and walls
- Added the Dyedream Sandstone building set: Dyedream Sandstone, Cut / Chiseled / Smooth Dyedream Sandstone, and their matching stairs, slabs and walls
- Added the Slumber Palm Block (the standing form supports 16-way rotation, the wall form supports four-way); Slumber Palm Blocks hang randomly from canopy branches, with a dedicated block-entity renderer
- Added the Wind Moor Sapling and its potted variant; Wind Moor Leaves are now ordinary decayable leaves that drop saplings, and canopies can be decorated with fig vines and hanging leaves
- Shadow Bookshelves now provide enchantment power (active within two blocks of an enchanting table), and Shadow Fungus has been added to the mushroom tag
> **Note**: Slumber Palm Blocks are not part of world generation and cannot be obtained in survival mode.

**Mechanics & Effects**
- Added the Cook Overflow mechanic: you can still eat even at full hunger; overflowed nutrition is converted into health / yellow hearts, with the cap rising with the cook level
- Implemented removal protection for protected effects; the Evasion effect is included and can no longer be cleared by ordinary means
- Added sanity change-rate sync and an arrow display for SAN changes, toggleable in the client config
- Added AI behavior for shadow mobs to stay away from campfires
- While the Nameless entity is in dialogue, other players interacting with it are now told that it is busy
- Crafting-type blocks now attempt a craft on the rising edge of an external redstone signal
- New config options: the Dyedream World Leapstone toggle `dyedreamWorldLeapstoneEnabled` (off by default; the Leapstone recipe is governed by it) and the JEI fluid display toggle `showFluidsInJei`

**World & Dimensions**
- Implemented rain (black rain) in the Lamp Shadow World dimension
- Added a KubeJS dream-dimension plugin: lets you define whether a dimension is a dream dimension, and supports removing / checking dream dimensions

**Skills & Tools**
- Added the Blink-loaded crossbow function to the Fractured Angel Statue: using Blink immediately reloads the crossbow in your main hand
- Added the Debug Tool (the creative-mode "Debug" tab): edit the NBT and inventory / fluid / energy storage of blocks, entities and items in-game, with page navigation and charge / discharge support

**Loot & Compatibility**
- Defined a brand-new loot table for Dyedream Campsites, added 3 tooltips, and added Traveler's Titles compatibility
- The Prophecy Card of Sin now judges illagers better: it can damage all illager-type entities for compatibility with other mods, and its instakill list now includes the Villager Soul from Illage and Spillage

### Balance
- Terra Blade supports Shift+Right-click to toggle the sword-qi state
- Brooch of White Orchid and Seal of the Corrupted now implement the ISanModifier interface
- The Swift Strike enchantment now grants different bonus ratios for swords and axes

### Fixed
- Fixed a NBT null-pointer exception when crafting in the Weapon Workshop
- Fixed the noise settings of the Lamp Shadow World dimension
- Fixed wrong notes in the Shadow Note Ruins and the Dream Train
- Fixed misaligned Storage Bag slots and two Storage Bags missing ItemHandler
- Fixed weapon combat arts disabling the Looting enchantment on entity loot
- Fixed Mortar fluid-container compatibility (missing JEI recipes and a ClassCastException)
- Fixed the target selector config of the Friendly Shadow Ghost entity
- Fixed the repair logic of the Blessing of Cecilia (non-creative consumption and client / server sync)
- Fixed the portal screen overlay texture display and flickering when toggling
- Fixed the biome check logic of the mob-spawn event, restoring Pink Sheep spawning
- Corrected the tooltip color of the Soul Gem
- Fixed the volume of the `sword_wave` sound effect
- Added the mod's sands to the sand tag
- Updated mob bestiary entries and advancement links
- Fixed NBT edits reading from a cache

### Refactored
- Refactored the Pink Mushroom block structure, merging the cap and stem into a single block
- Moved the client network handler logic into a dedicated handler class
- Refactored the dream-dimension helper's dimension-set initialization to allow external modification

### Docs & Standards
- Updated the content standards (license) terms
- Updated the ID mapping table, asset manifest, and architecture / development standards documents
