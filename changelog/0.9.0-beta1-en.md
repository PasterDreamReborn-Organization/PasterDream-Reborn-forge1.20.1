# Paster Dream Reborn v0.9.0-beta1 (Forge 1.20.1)

Version: `0.9.0-forge-1.20.1-beta1`

## Overview

This update focuses on the Lamp Shadow and Wind Journey endgame progression lines, adding full Shadow Alloy and Fluffy Wind Alloy equipment sets, the Shadow Dungeon Repository Layer, and the Moon House structure, along with the Melt Dream Bow, Fluffy Wind Bow, several special arrows, and four new enchantments. It also reworks the combat arts of Kusanagi, the Beihai Ruo Tide Sword, the Inferno Sword, and the Terra Blade, adjusts the Shelter enchantment and Melt Dream Energy regeneration, and fixes a batch of issues involving Melt Dream Energy, slow falling, Confusion, and combat-art checks.

### Added

**Shadow Alloy Series**
- Added Shadow Alloy Ingot, smelted in the Shadow Blast Furnace from Black Metal Ingots, Pure Horror, and Shadow Liquid
- Added the Shadow Alloy Pickaxe, Shadow Alloy Axe, Shadow Alloy Shovel, and Shadow Alloy Hoe, upgraded from the corresponding Shadow Erosion tools at a crafting table with enchantments preserved and durability reset to full
- Shadow Alloy tools inherit the Shadow Erosion bonuses against shadow-type blocks and in low light; in the main hand they grant +1 block reach and -1.2 Sanity Variability
- Shadow Alloy tools increase mining speed and attack damage as Sanity falls, up to +50%
- Shadow Alloy tools consume 0.1 Sanity to repair 1 durability while in the inventory
- Added the Shadow Alloy Helmet, Shadow Alloy Chestplate, Shadow Alloy Leggings, and Shadow Alloy Boots, upgraded from Titanium armor at the Weapon Workshop
- Each Shadow Alloy armor piece grants +4 max health and +1 attack damage
- A full Shadow Alloy armor set grants "Dark Watch": Lamp Shadow weapons deal +30% damage, taking a hit grants a 10-second Counterattack (30-second cooldown), and attacks from shadow mobs deal 50% less final damage
- Added the Shadow Alloy Crossbow, which consumes 0.2 Melt Dream Energy on firing to trigger "Shadow Flying Arrow"; it still fires normally without triggering the passive when energy is insufficient
- Shadow Alloy Crossbow hits apply 10 seconds of Vulnerability and a random 5-second debuff
- The Shadow Alloy Crossbow has built-in Power III that stacks with the Power enchantment, and it can receive Power at an enchanting table or anvil
- The Shadow Alloy Crossbow can receive Infinity; loading then consumes no normal arrows, while tipped arrows, spectral arrows, and fireworks are still consumed normally
- When the Shadow Alloy Crossbow is loaded with fireworks containing a firework star, creatures hit by the explosion also receive Vulnerability and the random debuff
- Bolts that trigger Shadow Flying Arrow leave a dark-purple trail

**Fluffy Wind & Wind Runner Series**
- Added the Fluffy Wind Alloy Helmet, Fluffy Wind Alloy Chestplate, Fluffy Wind Alloy Leggings, and Fluffy Wind Alloy Boots
- Each Fluffy Wind Alloy armor piece grants +5% movement speed and +5% combat-art cooldown reduction
- A full Fluffy Wind Alloy armor set grants "Boon of Wind and Thunder": -25% bow draw and crossbow load time, immunity to lightning damage, and doubled per-piece bonuses in non-clear weather
- "Boon of Wind and Thunder" grants 10% final damage reduction in rain, increased to 20% in thunderstorms
- "Boon of Wind and Thunder" gives a 50% chance to deflect projectiles that hit the player
- "Boon of Wind and Thunder" lets the player hold jump while falling to slow fall at the cost of hunger
- Added the Wind Runner Cloak, which occupies the chestplate slot and enables Elytra flight
- Holding jump while flying with the Wind Runner Cloak continuously boosts the player in the direction they are looking
- The Wind Runner Cloak grants immunity to kinetic damage from Elytra collisions
- Wearing the Wind Runner Cloak hides the vanilla cape

**Ranged Weapons & Arrows**
- Added the Fluffy Wind Bow, which consumes 0.2 Melt Dream Energy on firing to make that arrow ignore gravity
- Fluffy Wind Bow empowered arrows apply 1 second of Levitation on hit
- The Fluffy Wind Bow can receive Channeling; with it, each shot costs an additional 0.1 Melt Dream Energy, and empowered arrows strike lightning at the creatures they hit
- Added the Melt Dream Bow, which consumes 0.2 Melt Dream Energy on firing to deal +30% arrow damage as magic damage
- In the Dyedream World, the Melt Dream Bow consumes no arrows, durability, or Melt Dream Energy when firing normal arrows
- The Melt Dream Bow can receive Looting
- Melt Dream Bow empowered shots use a dedicated Melt Dream Arrow with a pink gradient trail
- Added the Blackstone Arrow, which deals 1.2x the damage of a normal arrow
- Added the Toxic Spore Arrow, which applies Highly Toxic on hit
- Added the Fragdream Arrow, which deals area damage within a 3-block radius when it hits a block or entity and cannot be picked up
- Added the Magical Arrow, which homes in on nearby targets in flight
- Added the Charged Arrow, which deals damage twice on hit, with one instance being magic damage that scales with draw charge
- The new arrows can be crafted or made at the Weapon Workshop, and they can be used as ammunition for bows and crossbows

**Curios & Food**
- Added the Iridescent Fairy Jade relic, which increases projectile speed by 50%
- Iridescent Fairy Jade makes the player's ranged projectiles apply Vulnerability II
- Iridescent Fairy Jade makes the player's ranged damage ignore invulnerability frames
- Iridescent Fairy Jade makes bows and crossbows fire an additional random projectile alongside the main shot
- Added the Evasion Cloak back-slot curio, obtainable from Melt Dream Crystal Chests and activated with a hotkey for 10 Melt Dream Energy
- When activated, the Evasion Cloak extends Blink's evasion time from 0.3 seconds to 0.6 seconds for 90 seconds
- A successful evasion with the Evasion Cloak grants Counterattack I for 10 seconds
- The Evasion Cloak has a 300-second cooldown
- Added the Molten Gold Apple, which grants 2 minutes of Fire Resistance and Haste II when eaten
- Added the Enchanted Molten Gold Apple, which grants 5 minutes of Fire Resistance, Haste IV, and Speed III, plus 20 seconds of Regeneration II when eaten

**Blocks & Building**
- Added Polished Cyan Stone, Polished Cyan Stone Stairs, Polished Cyan Stone Slab, and Polished Cyan Stone Wall; Polished Cyan Stone is made from Cyan Stone at a stonecutter, and the stairs, slab, and wall are cut from it

**Enchantments**
- Added the Gather Dream armor enchantment, which grants +0.25 Melt Dream Aura per level, up to level V
- Added the single-level Melt-Repair enchantment, which repairs an item's durability with Melt Dream Energy while it is in the inventory, armor, or curio slots
- Added the Curse of Heart-Devouring armor curse, which drains an equal amount of Sanity when the player takes damage, stacking independently for each cursed armor piece
- Added the single-level Heart-Devouring Repair curse, which repairs an item's durability with Sanity while it is in the inventory, armor, or curio slots
- Melt-Repair is mutually exclusive with Mending, and Heart-Devouring Repair is mutually exclusive with Mending and Melt-Repair

**World & Structures**
- Added the Moon House structure, generating in the snowy biomes of the Dyedream World about once every 256x256 chunks
- Added the Shadow Dungeon Repository Layer; after defeating Aaroncos, the bottom floor becomes the Repository Layer when the player re-enters the Shadow Dungeon
- Added Shadow Dungeon Repository loot, including core chests, common chests, and supply barrels, with Gold Ingots, Iron Ingots, Netherite Scrap, Echo Shards, Golden Apples, and a small chance for Shadow Alloy Ingots
- Added background music and ambient sounds to the Lamp Shadow biome

**Advancements & UI**
- Added the "Hundred Veils" challenge advancement for passing through Break Wind Curtains 100 times in total
- Added the "Angel Beats!" challenge advancement, which grants the Angel Wing after completing Riding the Wind, Advancing Against the Wind, and Hundred Veils
- Added a Paster Dream-themed player health bar, disabled by default, which can be enabled in the client config with a configurable number of decimal places
- Added the built-in "PasterDream · Vanilla UI" resource pack, which can replace some interface textures with the original mod's style
- Added JEI info page support with acquisition hints for the Murakumo Kusanagi, Pulse Wind Runner Crystal, Fractured Angel Statue, Sword Embryo, Lost Sword Tomb, Desert Greatsword, Chenjingmen Desert, Broken Hero Sword, Snow Vow, and Kaichu Omamori
- Added Patchouli entries for the Moon House, Sand of Time, Shadow Dungeon Repository Layer, Melt Dream Bow, Shadow Alloy Crossbow, Fluffy Wind Bow, and Windwreathed Thunder Spear

**Story & Notes**
- Added the story books "Dream Train", "Soul Gem In Current World", "Soul Gem In Current World (Fake)", "Records of the Mage III", "The Mage Builds a Home", and "The Pale Snow Lotus and the Pale Boneneedle"
- Added the "Dream Train" note to the Dream Train structure
- Added the Dyedream World Broken Note, Dyedream World Unknown Note, and Dyedream World Resolved Notes

### Changed

**Weapon Combat Arts**
- After enabling the sword-qi state on the Terra Blade, holding left-click now automatically fires sword-qi on cooldown
- Terra sword-qi now renders as a crescent-shaped blade light, decelerates linearly in flight, and has a random roll angle
- Kusanagi's combat art has been replaced with the passive "Venomous Stinger": each swing launches a venom stinger projectile, and hits stack a growing Highly Toxic effect
- Kusanagi's venom never reduces a target below 1 HP, but its stingers can execute poisoned targets left at 1 HP
- The Beihai Ruo Tide Sword can now be charged and thrown by sneaking and holding right-click, with the thrown projectile following vanilla trident logic for Loyalty, Channeling, and Impaling
- With Riptide, the Beihai Ruo Tide Sword cannot be thrown and instead charges a dash in water or rain
- The Beihai Ruo Tide Sword's passive has changed from Water Breathing to Conduit Power II
- The Beihai Ruo Tide Sword can now receive trident-exclusive enchantments at an enchanting table
- The Inferno Sword's combat art now triggers an area lava blast automatically on melee hits
- Each level of Fire Aspect on the Inferno Sword extends the passive burn by 2 seconds, and each level of Sweeping Edge increases the blast radius by 1 block (up to 6 blocks)
- Inferno Sword blast damage now benefits from Sharpness, Smite, and Bane of Arthropods
- The Counterattack effect now increases the player's next instance of damage dealt by 50% after damage reduction, instead of only boosting combat-art damage

**Items & Mechanics**
- Wind Spirit now grants +25% bow draw and crossbow load time reduction
- The Fractured Angel Statue no longer grants immunity to kinetic damage from Elytra collisions; this effect has moved to the Wind Runner Cloak
- Jungle Sporangium can now be bone-mealed to produce Jungle Spores
- Jungle Spores and Bone Meal can now be crafted into 2 Jungle Spores
- Added the missing crafting recipes for the Wind Moor Crate and Shadow Chest

**Visuals & Presentation**
- Added shader-pack sway support for vine-type blocks
- Redrew the textures for the Black Metal Block, Black Metal Grain, and Shadow Erosion tools
- Updated the textures for Qym's set, the White Sword, and the Terra Blade
- Redrew the texture for the Pale Bone Needle Talisman

**World & Biomes**
- Raised the Shadow Forest biome temperature from 0.2 to 0.5

### Balance

- Reworked the Shelter enchantment: damage reduction now scales with the total Shelter level across all worn armor at -2% per level; above 12 total levels it switches to an inverse-proportional formula that caps reduction at about 25%
- Increased Melt Dream Energy regeneration per Melt Dream Crystal armor piece from 0.2/min to 1/min
- The Melt Dream Energy Ring now regenerates 1 Melt Dream Energy per minute, up from 0.3
- The Melt Dream Crystal Chest Reset Tool now has a per-player 20-minute cooldown
- Using the Sand of Time now skips the Melt Dream Crystal Chest reset cooldown
- Added the Counter Ring to the Melt Dream Crystal Chest's level 2 rewards
- Added a Nether Star to the Pale Bone Needle Talisman recipe
- Raised the cumulative distance required for the Riding the Wind and Advancing Against the Wind challenges from 2,000 to 10,000 blocks
- The Terra Floating Island now grants +30% Terra Blade damage (including sword-qi) and reduces sword-qi Melt Dream Energy cost to 0.4
- The Terra Floating Island now makes Terra Blade melee attacks and sword-qi ignore invulnerability frames, and lets sword-qi pass through blocks

- Fixed Melt Dream Energy being lost after re-entering the world
- Fixed Melt Dream Energy being able to exceed its effective cap
- Fixed players being kicked for apparent flight after eating Galaxy Jelly or Milky Way Jelly
- Fixed Confusion pushing entities without a physics body into the void
- Fixed the Dyedream Perfume effect duration, from 1 minute to 10 minutes
- Fixed the multi-line description display for Dyedream Perfume
- Fixed intermittent Terra sword-qi trail particles
- Fixed client-side rendering lag and particle misalignment for Terra sword-qi
- Fixed lighting issues in White Sword Rain particles
- Fixed the excessively high volume of the Potion Bottle of Frozen
- Fixed the Beihai Ruo Tide Sword's Conduit Power triggering when selected in the off hand
- Fixed Ghost Face causing crashes when duplicating some modded gun bullets
- Fixed the fatal-damage interception priority between the Pale Bone Needle Talisman, the Totem of Undying, and the Blessing of Cecilia
- Fixed some combat arts still entering their charging state while wearing the Detective's Magnifying Glass
- Fixed some combat arts consuming resources repeatedly when right-clicking again after a miss

### Technical

- Extracted arrow behaviors into a reusable hook layer so weapon-empowered arrows can reuse custom arrow effects
- Unified shared combat-art cooldowns and skill-lock checks, and added charged-state validation
- Changed cooldown attributes from a multiplier to a reduction model and unified the related tooltips
- Separated Melt Dream Energy value changes from consumption checks
- Added a generic projectile trail renderer for Melt Dream Arrows, Shadow Alloy bolts, and Magical Arrows
- The loot generator now supports containers with item-handler capability but no item list in their NBT
- The debug tool now supports InteractAt interactions, client-side prediction fixes, and left-click entity deletion with spawn egg retrieval that preserves NBT
- Fixed an out-of-bounds issue in the NBT editor's multiline text box when refreshing
- Extracted minion-filtering logic into a shared utility class
- Added data generation for Shadow Dungeon Repository loot tables
