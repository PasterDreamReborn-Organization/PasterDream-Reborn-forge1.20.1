# Changelog

## v1.0.0 (Forge 1.20.1)

Version: `1.0.0-forge-1.20.1`

### Added

- Added automatic unlocking of all entries in the Seniors' Dream Guide while in Creative mode, ignoring story-progress locks, with a new "Unlock All Patchouli Entries in Creative Mode" option that is enabled by default

### Changed

- Updated the "Dreamseeker Notes" entry in the Seniors' Dream Guide with details about notes existing as fragments and being lootable from ruins and enemies
- Expanded the "Cook" entry in the Seniors' Dream Guide with the rules for eating while full and converting overflow hunger into health and yellow absorption hearts, and noted that the absorption cap rises with the effect's level
- Updated the Chinese cooldown reduction wording in the Cheer Up, Lethargy, and Trance entries of the Seniors' Dream Guide to "Combat Art cooldown reduction"
- Unified ellipsis and bracket usage in item descriptions, advancement text, and note text, using `...` and half-width brackets in English and `……` and full-width brackets in Chinese

### Fixed

- Fixed the mod's curio items being flagged as duplicates and returned to the inventory after reloading a save
- Fixed some Dream Church structures not displaying their names
- Fixed a typo in the Chinese descriptions of the "Power of the Jungle" and "Lost Souls Under the Sword" advancements

### Technical

- Added a language file validation tool that checks encoding, line endings, ellipses, brackets, and duplicate keys, and integrated it into the `check` task and CI
- Corrected the enchantment bonus description incorrectly stated in comments and design documentation related to Kusanagi Venom Stinger damage
