# Changelog

## v0.9.0-beta2 (Forge 1.20.1)

Version: `0.9.0-forge-1.20.1-beta2`

### Added

- Added the Blast Arrow, which explodes on impact with a block or entity, destroys blocks, and cannot be recovered
- Added a Weapon Workshop recipe for Blast Arrows using Blackstone Arrows, a Molten Gold Ingot, and Gunpowder
- Added the Dew Arrow, which ignores water resistance in flight and deals extra damage to mobs in water
- Added oxygen recovery to Dew Arrows, restoring one air bubble to the shooter when they hit a mob
- Added a crafting recipe for Dew Arrows using Blue Dew and normal arrows
- Added the Congeal Wind Arrow, which flies 30% faster than normal arrows and has built-in knockback and piercing effects
- Added a crafting recipe for Congeal Wind Arrows using Congeal Wind, a Stick, and Wind Feather Grass
- Added the Chaotic Arrow, which applies 3 seconds of Confusion on hitting a mob and deals extra damage to shadow mobs
- Added a chance for Chaotic Arrows to restore 0.5–2 Sanity to the shooter on hitting a mob, scaling with draw charge up to 30% at full charge
- Added support for Heart-Devouring Repair on Chaotic Arrows, allowing bows with this enchantment to fire them without consuming ammunition
- Added a Shadow Blast Furnace recipe for Chaotic Arrows using Blackstone Arrows, Nightmare Fuel, and Shadow Liquid

### Changed

- Added dispenser support for Blackstone Arrows, Toxic Spore Arrows, Fragdream Arrows, Magical Arrows, Charged Arrows, Blast Arrows, Dew Arrows, Congeal Wind Arrows, and Chaotic Arrows
- Added common Forge tags for metal ingots, nuggets, storage blocks, and ores so other mods can recognize these materials through the corresponding tags

### Balance

- Adjusted Aaroncos reward chests so reward quantities scale with the opening player's personal Shadow Difficulty; with the default configuration, each reward has a 50% chance to double on Normal and always doubles on Hard, while lower difficulties do not reduce rewards

### Fixed

- Fixed Ghost Face copies of mod arrows losing their special effects, preserving behaviors such as homing, area damage, poison, and enchantment-triggered lightning
- Fixed the Cook effect's overflow absorption cap being too low, correcting the caps for Cook I, II, and III to 20, 30, and 40 absorption health points, respectively
- Fixed the Sandwich recipe incorrectly requiring raw Chicken instead of Cooked Chicken
- Fixed a typo in the Chinese Kaichu Omamori tooltip's wording for the foxfire field
- Fixed the Dyedream Crack's portal cooldown: staying inside the crack during cooldown now refreshes it continuously, and the countdown only starts after leaving, preventing repeated teleportation while standing inside

### Technical

- Updated some Mixin injections to wrap existing behavior with MixinExtras and switched the associated member access to access transformers
- Updated the release build process so the default artifact bundles MixinExtras and is reobfuscated, while the artifact without bundled dependencies uses the -slim suffix
- Standardized internal names for Melt Dream Aurorian Steel and Black Metal Grain and added legacy item mappings to preserve existing items when upgrading worlds
